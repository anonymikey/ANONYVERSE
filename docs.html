ANONYVERSE documentation

One home for movies, shows, anime, live TV and live sports, built by ANONYMIKEY and hosted at anonymiketech.space.

What you get

Movies & shows

Popular, latest and upcoming titles, plus genres and categories.

Anime

Search-based anime catalogue with episode lists and subbed or dubbed audio when available.

Live TV

Kenyan channels plus a country picker for channels from around the world.

Live sports

Matches in progress, today’s fixtures and replays.

My List

Save anything you want to come back to. Stored on your device.

Continue watching

Pick up where you left off, right from the home page. Finished episodes roll on to the next one.

Smart suggestions

ANONYVERSE remembers what you search most and suggests related titles.

Always moving

The home banner and the Latest rows scroll on their own. Touch or hover to pause.

Made for every screen

Phones, tablets, laptops and big TVs, in portrait or landscape.

Browsing

  • Home: a featured title and rows for trending, live sports, live TV, popular and latest content.
  • Movies and Shows: switch between Popular, Latest and Upcoming, or pick a genre or category chip. Use Load more for more titles.
  • Anime: choose a popular name or use the search bar to find any anime.
  • Live TV: Kenyan channels first. Pick another country from the chips to see its channels.
  • Live Sports: Live now shows matches in progress, Today shows the day’s matches and Replays shows finished games.

On desktop the sidebar expands when you hover it. On phones it becomes a bottom bar.

Watching

  • Open a movie, show or anime to see its details: overview, rating, genres, the cast with their characters, and every episode.
  • For shows, pick a season, then tap an episode. Next episode appears in the player.
  • Pick a quality (1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p) for movies and shows. Lower qualities help on slow connections.
  • Use PiP for picture in picture, or Download on movies and shows where available.
  • Playback links are temporary. If you pause for a long time, ANONYVERSE fetches a fresh link and continues from the same spot.

My List

Tap the + on any card, or use the My List button on a details page. Tap again to remove it. Find everything under My List in the sidebar. Your list lives in your browser, so it stays on this device and is cleared if you clear site data.

Search

Press / to jump to the search bar, then type at least two letters. Results cover movies and shows, with anime shown in its own section. Press Esc to clear and go back.

  • Your top searches: tap the search bar to see what you search most, plus your recent searches. Pick one to search again. Use Clear search history to wipe them.
  • Suggestions: the home page shows Your most searched and Because you searched… rows. Search results end with You may also like.

Tips and fixes

  • Nothing plays: try another quality, refresh the page, or pick another title. Some titles aren’t available in every quality.
  • Live sports is empty: streams only appear while matches are available. Check back closer to kick-off.
  • Video stops after a long pause: press play. The link refreshes automatically.
  • Autoplay blocked: some browsers need one tap on Tap to play.
  • Back button: on phones, Back closes the player or details first, so you don’t leave the app by accident.
  • Install it: on a phone, use your browser’s Add to Home screen to get an app-style icon.

Privacy

ANONYVERSE has no accounts. Your My List, playback positions, Continue watching and search history are saved only in your own browser. Clearing your browser data removes them.
